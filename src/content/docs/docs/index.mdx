**Tunyx is a self-optimizing inference control plane.** Point it at a model, tell it your workload, and get a
fast, validated, OpenAI-compatible endpoint on your own hardware that stays healthy.

It sits between your applications and an inference engine (vLLM, SGLang, llama.cpp or MLX). It decides how to
run the engine for *your* workload, proves the result with benchmarks and a quality gate, and operates it day
to day.

```bash
tunyx up qwen2.5:7b --profile agent
```

## What you get

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Does the inference engineering" icon="rocket">

  <Card title="Gated on your benchmarks" icon="approve-check">

## Who it's for

- **Teams moving from managed APIs to open models** for cost, privacy or control, without hiring an inference
  team.
- **Teams already running vLLM by hand** who want the flag tuning, validation, swaps and on-call work automated.
- **Teams outgrowing Ollama** who need throughput under concurrency, quality checks and safe updates.

Tunyx manages a single node with up to 8 GPUs, MIG slices, Apple Silicon Macs or CPU servers.
